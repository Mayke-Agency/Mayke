Selected Work

Laguna Coffee

Social Strategy / Content

We helped turn Laguna Coffee Co. into one of the most recognizable social brands in its local market. Through a content strategy built around consistency, storytelling, and culture-first creative, we scaled the brand from videos reaching a few hundred viewers to consistent 5K–40K view performance, alongside multiple viral videos in the hundreds of thousands and one surpassing 5.7 million views.

Beyond growth, we helped redefine what social media looked like for local businesses in Laguna Beach. Through ongoing content production, creative direction, and graphic design, the brand built a loyal digital community, gained over 4,000 followers, and became widely known for its online presence both inside and outside the local business scene.

JN Traveler

Web / Branding

We created a fully custom digital experience for JN Traveler centered around elevated storytelling and intentional positioning within the luxury travel space. From web design to copywriting, every detail was crafted to feel refined, immersive, and highly personalized.

The goal was to move beyond a standard travel website and build a brand presence that communicates trust, exclusivity, and experience. Through clean editorial layouts, strategic messaging, and a tailored visual direction, we positioned the brand to better connect with high-intent luxury clientele.

Wigz

Content / Social Strategy

For Wigz, the focus was on building a brand presence with longevity. We established a stronger digital voice, clarified the visual direction, and developed a content approach centered around authenticity and audience connection rather than trend-dependent growth.

Since launch, engagement and video performance have steadily increased while the brand continues to cultivate a more engaged and recognizable online community. The work positioned Wigz with a clearer identity and a stronger foundation for long-term digital growth.

Mago Hot Sauce

Web / Branding / Content / Social Strategy

We led a complete rebrand of Mago Hot Sauce’s digital presence, redefining how the brand shows up across web, social, and online storytelling. The project included a full website redesign, social media strategy, creative direction, and ongoing content development aimed at positioning the brand with a stronger, more elevated identity while staying connected to its roots and community.

The new digital experience was designed to feel bold, culture-driven, and visually immersive — combining modern DTC design language with storytelling that highlights the personality behind the brand. Across social, we developed a more cohesive content strategy focused on engagement, brand recognition, and long-term audience growth, helping establish a clearer voice and stronger visual consistency across every touchpoint.

Coyote Grill

Social Strategy / Content

We repositioned Coyote Grill’s social presence from occasional posting into a consistent brand ecosystem designed to drive engagement, visibility, and community recognition. What once generated hundreds of views now regularly reaches 15K+ viewers, with multiple videos surpassing 20K and several reaching millions organically.

Our approach focused on building content that felt native to the culture around the brand — energetic, community-driven, and highly shareable. As the audience grew, so did guest interaction, shares, and overall brand awareness. Today, Coyote Grill has become one of the most socially recognizable restaurant brands in its local market.

Jete Dance Center

Web / Brand Presence

We redesigned Jeté Dance Studio’s digital experience to better reflect the professionalism and energy of the studio itself. The project included full web design, brand consultation, and content direction, alongside the development of a custom student registration and enrollment system designed to streamline onboarding for families and staff.

The new platform modernized the studio’s online presence while improving usability across both mobile and desktop. Beyond aesthetics, the focus was creating a system that supports growth, simplifies operations, and gives the studio a more elevated and cohesive brand experience online.

Daughter of Gaia

Brand / Identity

We developed Daughter of Gaia as a fully realized brand system from the ground up — spanning brand identity, positioning, packaging, web design, and creative direction. Every element was built to create a cohesive, elevated presence within the wellness space while maintaining a sense of warmth and emotional connection.

The project focused heavily on world-building: creating a brand that feels intentional at every touchpoint, from packaging and digital experience to tone and visual storytelling. Following launch, the partnership will expand into strategy, content, and ongoing brand growth initiatives.
