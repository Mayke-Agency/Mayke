Creator Network

How it works

Mayke connects brands and businesses with creators who can help bring their stories, spaces, products, and experiences to life. Our Creator Network is a growing community of content creators we partner with for select client campaigns, from social content and UGC to local experiences, events, product launches, and brand collaborations.

If you’re a creator interested in working with businesses that fit your style and audience, we’d love to know you. Join the Mayke Creator Network to be considered for upcoming opportunities, including paid partnerships, gifted products, hosted experiences, and other collaborations. When we have an opportunity that feels like the right fit, we’ll reach out with the details.
